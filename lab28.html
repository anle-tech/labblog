Lab 28 Design and Implement a VLSM Addressing Scheme

By An Le

In lab 28, we will design a VLSM addressing scheme from a given /24 network, we will assign subnets and addressing, configure devices and verify connectivity.

Part 1: Examine the Network Requirements

Step 1: Determine the number of subnets needed.

Based on the information given, we will need 5 subnets.

Step 2: Determine the subnet mask information for each subnet.

Each subnet must provide enough hosts to cover network and broadcast address as well as host IP address requirement.

Subnet Mask Useable Requirement
Room-114 255.255.255.240 14 14
Room-279 255.255.255.224 30 30
Room-312 255.255.255.255.248 6 6
Room-407 255.255.255.192 62 60
To connect Branch1 and Branch2, we only need 255.255.255.252 /30.
